# Sources for the cash-flow and FX-hedging proposal (v2)

Twelve cited works. Original-proposal files and manifest are preserved. Colons in titles are rendered as spaced hyphens. Filename year is publication year; the version column discloses earlier manuscripts.

- [Principles and Algorithms for Forecasting Groups of Time Series: Locality and Globality (2021)](Principles%20and%20Algorithms%20for%20Forecasting%20Groups%20of%20Time%20Series%20-%20Locality%20and%20Globality%20Montero-Manso%282021%29.pdf). Author manuscript; journal citation 2021.
- [DeepAR: Probabilistic Forecasting with Autoregressive Recurrent Networks (2020)](DeepAR%20-%20Probabilistic%20Forecasting%20with%20Autoregressive%20Recurrent%20Networks%20Salinas%282020%29.pdf). arXiv v3, 22 February 2019, three authors; journal 2020 lists Januschowski as fourth author.
- [Policy Learning with Observational Data (2021)](Policy%20Learning%20with%20Observational%20Data%20Athey%282021%29.pdf). arXiv accepted manuscript, 2020; journal citation 2021.
- [A New Approach to Linear Filtering and Prediction Problems (1960)](A%20New%20Approach%20to%20Linear%20Filtering%20and%20Prediction%20Problems%20Kalman%281960%29.pdf). Transcription of published paper.
- [An Approach to Time Series Smoothing and Forecasting Using the EM Algorithm (1982)](An%20Approach%20to%20Time%20Series%20Smoothing%20and%20Forecasting%20Using%20the%20EM%20Algorithm%20Shumway%281982%29.pdf). Published scan, OCR sidecar.
- [Forecasting Using Principal Components from a Large Number of Predictors (2002)](Forecasting%20Using%20Principal%20Components%20from%20a%20Large%20Number%20of%20Predictors%20Stock%282002%29.pdf). Published paper.
- [Deep State Space Models for Time Series Forecasting (2018)](Deep%20State%20Space%20Models%20for%20Time%20Series%20Forecasting%20Rangapuram%282018%29.pdf). Official long version extracted from NeurIPS supplementary ZIP.
- [Causal Inference in Statistics - An Overview (2009)](Causal%20Inference%20in%20Statistics%20-%20An%20Overview%20Pearl%282009%29.pdf). Author technical report of published survey.
- [The Determinants of Firms' Hedging Policies (1985)](The%20Determinants%20of%20Firms%27%20Hedging%20Policies%20Smith%281985%29.pdf). Published scan, OCR sidecar.
- [Risk Management - Coordinating Corporate Investment and Financing Policies (1993)](Risk%20Management%20-%20Coordinating%20Corporate%20Investment%20and%20Financing%20Policies%20Froot%281993%29.pdf). NBER working paper 4084, May 1992; journal publication 1993.
- [Conditional Logit Analysis of Qualitative Choice Behavior (1974)](Conditional%20Logit%20Analysis%20of%20Qualitative%20Choice%20Behavior%20McFadden%281974%29.pdf). Published chapter scan, OCR sidecar.
- [Mixed MNL Models for Discrete Response (2000)](Mixed%20MNL%20Models%20for%20Discrete%20Response%20McFadden%282000%29.pdf). Author manuscript revised May 15, 2000.
