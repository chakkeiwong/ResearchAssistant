# Industrial Research SOP

## Required Sections

- paper intake;
- source and PDF validation;
- derivation review;
- experiment evidence;
- parser benchmark gate;
- traceability review;
- LLM/provider use;
- readiness approval;
- support and escalation;
- release approval.

## Approval

This SOP requires a department owner, reviewers, approval date, expiration date,
and review cadence before it can unblock departmental beta or production.
