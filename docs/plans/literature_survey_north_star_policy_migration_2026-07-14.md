# North-Star Program Governance Migration

Date: `2026-07-14`
Status: `ACTIVE`

The current repository `AGENTS.md` supersedes the older M17-M23 setup language
that required special hash-bound approval wording and review of every control
artifact before ordinary trusted-local work. Historical setup and approval
artifacts remain preserved as evidence; they will not be regenerated or used
as active execution tokens.

Active governance is proportionate to a trusted local academic repository:

- M18 uses one concise reviewed integration plan, exact Git/path provenance,
  non-destructive commits, isolated clean-install validation, ordinary
  checksums, a bounded attempt/time budget, and one terminal result review.
- The user's repeated `execute` and crash-resume requests authorize this local
  campaign. No additional magic wording is required for staging, committing,
  local cloning, wheel building, `/tmp` environments, or CPU-only tests.
- A localized harness, packaging, serialization, or dependency-closure failure
  may be repaired and retried within the unchanged M18 contract and budget,
  using fresh versioned outputs and preserving prior attempts.
- Claude or fresh Codex review is advisory. Reviewer unavailability or a purely
  procedural objection does not override passing local evidence.
- Explicit human authority remains required at the actual boundary for push or
  public release, destructive/history-rewriting action, credentials, privacy
  changes, paid or materially expanded compute, material product/scientific
  direction, M19-M21 live/network/source work, and M22 genuine human decisions.

This migration changes procedure only. It does not establish clean-checkout
reproducibility, live behavior, source support, human review, scientific
correctness, product readiness, release readiness, or north-star completion.
