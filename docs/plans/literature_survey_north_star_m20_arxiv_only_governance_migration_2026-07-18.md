Date: `2026-07-18`

## Human Override

The user explicitly overrode the active M20/G3 exit criteria. OpenAlex and all
credentialed citation providers are unavailable and permanently outside the
active project scope. They must not be requested, treated as prerequisites, or
attempted again.

This migration is governed by the current repository policy. It does not pass
through the retired provider-review or approval-token machinery.

## Historical Preservation

All earlier provider-dependent M20/M20A/M20B plans, packets, runners, results,
and validation roots remain unchanged historical evidence. Their facts about
past attempts remain valid, but their provider routes, credentials, costs,
approval tokens, packet hashes, and forward-citation promotion predicates are
not active execution authority.

## Revised G3 Definition

G3 is renamed from `live citation discovery` to `live source-grounded backward
discovery`. It asks whether one exact public seed source can produce a safe,
replayable, classified backward-reference candidate set with explicit omission
risks.

M20 may close when all of these hold:

1. exact seed identity is bound to retained local metadata;
2. one authorized public arXiv source package is retained within the numeric
   source and total-root caps;
3. archive path/type/member/expansion checks pass without filesystem extraction;
4. at least one canonical DOI/arXiv backward-reference candidate is extracted;
5. every candidate has an explicit classification state and next action;
6. identifier-free/parser-recall, technical-support, retraction/version, and
   source-scope omissions are recorded;
7. forward coverage is recorded as `unavailable_out_of_scope`, non-blocking,
   and never represented as zero or complete;
8. retained-body offline replay passes and raw source remains confined to its
   validation root.

## Scientific Boundary

This revised M20 can establish source intake and bounded backward candidate
discovery. It cannot establish forward-citation coverage, literature
completeness, technical relevance, source correctness, retraction safety,
scientific validity, or prose readiness. Those remain explicit limitations or
later review/source-anchor work.

## Handoff

A revised-contract pass closes M20/G3 and hands the retained seed source plus
candidate/omission ledgers to M21. M21 may inspect technical anchors and source
status under its own active plan, but no provider-dependent forward expansion
is reintroduced.
