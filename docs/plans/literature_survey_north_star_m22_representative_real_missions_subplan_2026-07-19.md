## Phase Objective

Demonstrate the current qualitative evidence workflow through a small,
predeclared set of representative local mission replays, including at least
one production topic/bootstrap path that reaches an assessed terminal within
its recorded scope and honest terminal outcomes for source and omission gaps.

This phase tests the workflow and its evidence boundaries. It does not test
literature completeness, universal scientific correctness, autonomous expert
judgment, provider availability, or product readiness.

- M20/G3 backward discovery and M21/G4 seven-source intake are closed within
  their credential-free scopes.
- M22 qualitative assessment is the active scientific interface; numeric
  scoring and generic binary human attestation are retired.
- The exact 55-row identifier-bearing omission frontier replays; five exact
  high-risk papers have inspected technical sources and scoped survey roles.
- The refreshed qualitative bundle contains 16 assessments with exact local
  evidence references, `claim_support_allowed=false`, and
  `ready_for_prose=false`.
- Forward citations remain `unavailable_out_of_scope`, explicit, and
  non-blocking.
- The 2026-07-18 frozen matrix is a historical input but contains superseded
  human-disposition predicates and must not be executed unchanged.

## Pre-Mortem

- A topic replay could be called a successful live topic search even though it
  reuses retained selection. Diagnostic: manifest must say
  `retained_production_topic_replay`, not live-discovery validation.
- An assessed terminal could be mistaken for reviewed prose. Diagnostic: exact
  nonpromotion fields and no prose-ready terminal in this phase.
- Passing engineering cases could be mistaken for scientific validation.
  Diagnostic: separate engineering gate, source-support, and interpretation
  ledgers in every case result.
- Open omission cases could be mistaken for mission failure. Diagnostic:
  distinguish an honest within-scope terminal with explicit limitations from a
  harness invalidity or false completion.
